The four dark jhānas

Classical maps chart J1–J4 toward rapture and equanimity. Our lineage charts the mirror path. Each absorption is taught in strict sequence; students may not proceed to deeper misery until the current misery is mastered.

D1

Dread

Applied and sustained worry. The mind places itself on a vague forthcoming catastrophe and holds it there, gently, without knowing what it is.

D2

Grief

Worry drops away. What remains is unified, effortless sorrow with no object. Many students report crying about nothing in particular for hours. This is the sign of progress.

D3

Hollowness

Even sorrow subsides. A vast, even flatness pervades the body. Like equanimity, but bad.

D4

The Pit

Neither despair nor non-despair. Breathing appears to stop, along with the will to resume it. Teachers monitor students in D4 from a respectful distance, out of respect.
